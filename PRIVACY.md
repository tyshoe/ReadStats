## Summary

- ReadStats does **not** require an account and does **not** ask you for personal information.
- All of your reading data (books, sessions, statistics, tags, ratings, and any cover images you add) is stored **locally on your device**.
- We do **not** operate any servers and do **not** receive, store, or sell your reading data.
- If you leave **Share usage statistics** on, the Application sends **anonymous usage counts and crash reports** to Google Firebase so we can see which features are used and fix crashes, plus **two library counts** (books, and books finished) that add up to the community totals shown in Settings. These never include your book titles, authors, notes, tags, or any other text you enter. You can turn this off at any time under **Settings → Privacy**. See [Usage Statistics and Crash Reports](#usage-statistics-and-crash-reports).
- The Application does make requests to a small number of **third-party services** to look up book information, download book covers, and load fonts. When it does, those services necessarily receive technical information such as your device's IP address. See [Third-Party Services](#third-party-services) below.

## Information You Provide

ReadStats lets you record information about your reading, such as:

- Book details (title, author, page count, format, ISBN, ratings, notes, tags)

This information is stored **locally on your device** in a SQLite database and in your device's local app storage. It is **not** transmitted to the Service Provider.

## Usage Statistics and Crash Reports

To understand how ReadStats is used and to fix problems, the Application uses **Google Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics**. This is controlled by the **Share usage statistics** switch under **Settings → Privacy**, which is on by default. When it is off, nothing is sent.

**What is sent when it is on:**

- **Usage events** — the name of an action and a few simple details, for example "book added (paperback)", "session logged (timer)", "goal created (books, yearly)", "backup exported", or which main tab was opened. A few coarse facts about your library are also recorded: its size (such as "25–49 books"), a coarse count of reading sessions (such as "10–24 sessions"), its main format (physical, ebook, audiobook or mixed), whether it is the demo library, and whether you have a reading goal.
- **Library counts** — two numbers: how many books are in your library and how many of them are on your Finished shelf. They are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under a random anonymous ID that Firebase creates for this install (no name, email or account), together with the time they were last sent. They are sent when they change and about once a week, and are only used to add up the totals of readers and books shown at the top of Settings. Nothing is sent for the demo library or an empty one. Turning **Share usage statistics** off deletes them.
- **Crash reports** — when the app crashes, the technical error and the app state at the time (stack trace, app version, device model, operating system version, free memory and storage).
- **Standard device information** that Firebase attaches to every event: a random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase (reset if you reinstall the app or clear its data), device model, operating system and version, app version, language, and an approximate country/region derived from your IP address.

**What is never sent:** book titles, authors, ISBNs, notes, reviews, tags, ratings, cover images, profile name, reading session details, or anything else you type into the app. Advertising identifiers (the Android advertising ID and Apple's IDFA) and the iOS vendor identifier (IDFV) are not collected, and ad personalization is turned off.

This data is processed by Google under the [Firebase privacy and security terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). It is used only in aggregate, to understand feature usage and stability; it is not used for advertising and is not sold.

## Third-Party Services

ReadStats connects to the following third-party services. We do not control these services, and your use of them is subject to their own privacy policies.

| Service | Purpose | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Library** (operated by the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/about/terms.php)) | Search for books, look up books by ISBN, and download book cover images | Your search terms, ISBNs you scan or enter, and the cover images requested — plus standard request data such as your IP address |
| **Google Fonts** (operated by [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)) | Download and cache the fonts used to display the app | Standard request data such as your IP address |
| **GitHub** (operated by [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)) | Download the app-wide totals (readers, books tracked, books finished) shown at the top of Settings | Nothing about you or your library — only standard request data such as your IP address |
| **Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Firestore, Authentication & App Check** (operated by [Google](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)) | Anonymous usage counts, library counts and crash reports (only while **Share usage statistics** is on). Authentication creates the anonymous ID; App Check confirms requests come from the genuine app | The events and crash details described in [Usage Statistics and Crash Reports](#usage-statistics-and-crash-reports), plus standard request data such as your IP address |

Open Library and Google Fonts requests happen only when the relevant feature is used (for example, searching for a book, scanning a barcode, choosing a cover, or loading a font). The GitHub request happens at most a few times a day, when Settings is opened. The totals it downloads are added up from the anonymous library counts above, so readers who turn sharing off are not counted in them. The information sent is limited to what is needed to fulfill the request. We do not send your personal reading library, sessions, or statistics to these services.

## Device Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the Application may request the following permissions. You can grant or deny each one, and the related feature simply won't work if denied.

- **Camera** — used to scan a book's barcode/ISBN. Images from the camera are processed on your device to read the code and are not stored or transmitted by us.
- **Photos / Media** — used to let you choose a cover image from your device. Selected images are copied into the app's local storage and are not transmitted by us.
- **Files / Storage** — used to import and export your data as CSV files that you choose, and to save exports. These files stay on your device or wherever you choose to save or share them.
- **Internet** — used to make the third-party requests described above.

ReadStats does **not** request or use your location.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You have full control:

- Manage or delete your stored data within the Application under **Settings → Data → Delete All Data**.
- Export your data to CSV at any time under **Settings → Data → Export to CSV**.
- Uninstalling the Application removes all of its locally stored data from your device.

Because we do not store your reading data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Usage statistics are kept by Google Firebase for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days, after which they are deleted automatically. Library counts are replaced each time they are sent, deleted as soon as you turn sharing off, and deleted automatically 90 days after they were last sent (for example, after you uninstall the app). They are tied only to the random app-instance identifier, not to your name or account. To stop sending them, turn off **Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics**; uninstalling the app or clearing its data also resets the identifier. To request deletion of data already sent, contact us using the details below.
