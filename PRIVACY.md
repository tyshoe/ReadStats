# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 28, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 28, 2026

ReadStats ("Application", "we", "us") was created by **Tyler Shoemake** ("Service Provider") as a free app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Application handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Application is provided **AS IS**.

By using ReadStats, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## Summary

- ReadStats does **not** require an account and does **not** ask you for personal information.
- All of your reading data (books, sessions, statistics, tags, ratings, and any cover images you add) is stored **locally on your device**.
- We do **not** operate any servers and do **not** receive, store, or sell your data.
- The Application does make requests to a small number of **third-party services** to look up book information, download book covers, and load fonts. When it does, those services necessarily receive technical information such as your device's IP address. See [Third-Party Services](#third-party-services) below.

---

## Information You Provide

ReadStats lets you record information about your reading, such as:

- Book details (title, author, page count, format, ISBN, ratings, notes, tags)
- Reading sessions and progress
- Cover images you select from your device or download from a book lookup
- App preferences (theme, fonts, default formats, etc.)

This information is stored **locally on your device** in a SQLite database and in your device's local app storage. It is **not** transmitted to the Service Provider.

---

## Information Collected Automatically

The Service Provider does **not** collect analytics, usage data, advertising identifiers, or location data, and does not operate any backend that receives your information.

However, like any app that connects to the internet, when the Application contacts a third-party service (see below) that service may automatically receive standard technical request data, including your **IP address**, the request you made, and basic device/network information. This is inherent to making an internet request and is handled by those third parties under their own privacy policies — not by us.

---

## Third-Party Services

ReadStats connects to the following third-party services. We do not control these services, and your use of them is subject to their own privacy policies.

| Service | Purpose | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Library** (operated by the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/about/terms.php)) | Search for books, look up books by ISBN, and download book cover images | Your search terms, ISBNs you scan or enter, and the cover images requested — plus standard request data such as your IP address |
| **Google Fonts** (operated by [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)) | Download and cache the fonts used to display the app | Standard request data such as your IP address |

These requests happen only when the relevant feature is used (for example, searching for a book, scanning a barcode, choosing a cover, or loading a font). The information sent is limited to what is needed to fulfill the request. We do not send your personal reading library, sessions, or statistics to these services.

---

## Device Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the Application may request the following permissions. You can grant or deny each one, and the related feature simply won't work if denied.

- **Camera** — used to scan a book's barcode/ISBN. Images from the camera are processed on your device to read the code and are not stored or transmitted by us.
- **Photos / Media** — used to let you choose a cover image from your device. Selected images are copied into the app's local storage and are not transmitted by us.
- **Files / Storage** — used to import and export your data as CSV files that you choose, and to save exports. These files stay on your device or wherever you choose to save or share them.
- **Internet** — used to make the third-party requests described above.

ReadStats does **not** request or use your location.

---

## How Your Information Is Used

Information is used solely to:

- Provide the app's features (tracking books and sessions, showing statistics)
- Look up book details and covers when you ask it to
- Save and restore your preferences
- Import and export data at your request

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the app.

---

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

The Service Provider does **not** sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. The only data that leaves your device is the limited request information sent to the third-party services listed above, and any data **you** choose to export or share (for example, sharing an exported CSV file through your device's share sheet).

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You have full control:

- Manage or delete your stored data within the Application under **Settings → Data → Delete All Data**.
- Export your data to CSV at any time under **Settings → Data → Export to CSV**.
- Uninstalling the Application removes all of its locally stored data from your device.

Because we do not store your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

## International Users

The third-party services referenced above may process requests on servers located in the United States or other countries. By using features that contact those services, you understand that the related request data may be processed in those locations under the respective providers' privacy policies.

ReadStats is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in parts of the EU). The Application does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because no personal data is collected by the Service Provider, none is knowingly collected from children. ## Security

Your reading data is stored locally on your device and is protected by your device's own security (such as your device passcode or biometrics). Because we do not transmit or store your data on our own servers, it is not exposed to server-side breaches on our part. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Because ReadStats stores your data only on your device and the Service Provider does not collect or hold it, you can exercise these rights directly at any time by viewing, editing, exporting, or deleting your data within the app. For any questions, contact us using the details below.
